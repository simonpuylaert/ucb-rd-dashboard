5-MINUTE CLIENT WALKTHROUGH

How to demonstrate the solution

This concept connects the expected dashboard to the less visible data and process foundations needed to sustain it.

  1. Overview: change therapeutic area and observe KPIs, milestones and risks update.
  2. Programme explorer: select an at-risk programme to open the detail and quality signals.
  3. Decision simulator: change delay or capacity assumptions and compare impacts.
  4. Data & governance: show traceability, checks, owners and the proposed operating model.
  5. Export: download a filtered dataset or a portfolio review briefing.
Important caveat
Every dataset, programme, status, metric and modeled relationship is illustrative and fictional. Data sources are proposed examples, not confirmed integrations.
